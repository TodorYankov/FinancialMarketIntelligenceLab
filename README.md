# FinancialMarketIntelligenceLab

An exploratory Data Science research project investigating the relationships between Bitcoin (BTC-USD), the S&P 500 index, the US interest-rate environment, and public search interest in Bitcoin.

## Research question

How do Bitcoin and S&P 500 returns and volatility vary over time, and how are they associated with changes in the interest-rate environment and public interest in Bitcoin?

## Research objectives

- Explore historical financial time series and daily returns.
- Assess data quality, coverage, consistency, and missing observations.
- Investigate returns, volatility, and relationships between financial variables.
- Examine public search interest in Bitcoin as an additional analytical variable.
- Integrate financial and search interest data into a monthly research dataset.
- Apply statistical hypothesis testing to financial research questions.
- Communicate findings through visualizations, statistical evidence, and explicit limitations.

## Data sources

| Source | Series | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/) | BTC-USD | Bitcoin prices in US dollars |
| [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/) | ^GSPC | S&P 500 price index |
| [FRED / Federal Reserve Board](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10) | DGS10 | 10-year Treasury constant-maturity market yield, percent per annum |
| [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/) | Bitcoin | Relative search interest in Bitcoin |
| [Bitstamp](https://www.bitstamp.net/) | BTC/USD | Historical Bitcoin market prices |
| [Coin Metrics](https://coinmetrics.io/) | BTC | Historical Bitcoin market data |

The Yahoo Finance data were downloaded through `yfinance`, with coverage from January 2018 to November 2026. Exact coverage and download details are documented in the notebooks.

Raw data are stored locally in `data/raw/`.

Earlier exploratory inputs include historical Bitcoin price data and Binance BTC/USDT OHLCV data. BTC/USDT is distinct from BTC-USD. These preliminary inputs are not silently combined with the USD analysis.

### Processed dataset

`data/processed/market_monthly_2018_2026.csv`

An integrated monthly research dataset generated by the market data validation notebook and used as the input for statistical hypothesis testing.

The dataset integrates information from four main research variables:

- Bitcoin (BTC-USD).
- S&P 500 (^GSPC).
- US 10-year Treasury yield (DGS10).
- Google Trends Bitcoin search interest.

## Research workflow

| Stage | Notebook | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | [Data Exploration](notebooks/01_data_exploration.ipynb) | Initial exploration, descriptive analysis, daily returns, and data acquisition |
| 02 | [Market Data Validation](notebooks/02_market_data_validation.ipynb) | Data quality, validation, temporal alignment, and monthly dataset preparation |
| 03 | [Hypothesis Testing](notebooks/03_hypothesis_testing.ipynb) | Statistical hypothesis testing and interpretation |

The notebooks form a progressive research workflow, from data exploration and validation to statistical inference.

### Stage 01 - Data Exploration

- Historical financial data exploration.
- Descriptive statistics.
- Daily return calculations.
- Preliminary OHLCV data analysis.
- Financial data acquisition.
- Cross-source comparison of Bitcoin historical prices.
- Analysis of price deviations between data providers.
- Comparative analysis of returns and volatility.

### Stage 02 - Market Data Validation

- Data quality assessment.
- Missing observation analysis.
- Historical coverage assessment.
- Temporal alignment of multiple data sources.
- Monthly dataset preparation.

### Stage 03 - Hypothesis Testing

- Formulation of statistical research questions.
- Null and alternative hypotheses.
- Significance levels.
- Statistical hypothesis testing.
- Confidence intervals.
- Interpretation of statistical evidence.
- Discussion of assumptions and limitations.

The statistical methodology considers null and alternative hypotheses, significance levels, p-values, confidence intervals, test assumptions, and practical interpretation.

The choice of statistical test depends on the research question and the properties of the data.

## Running the notebooks

From the project directory, install the baseline dependencies:

```bash
python -m pip install pandas numpy matplotlib seaborn yfinance scipy notebook
```

Launch Jupyter:

```bash
cd notebooks
jupyter notebook
```

Run the notebooks in numerical order:

1. `01_data_exploration.ipynb`
2. `02_market_data_validation.ipynb`
3. `03_hypothesis_testing.ipynb`

Some preliminary sections require earlier input files. Ensure that the required datasets are available in `data/raw/`.

The second notebook generates the processed monthly dataset required by the third notebook.

The dependency list is a starting point rather than a verified, version-pinned environment. Reproducibility will be improved during development.

## Limitations

- This is an exploratory educational research project, not financial advice.
- Statistical associations do not establish causation.
- Bitcoin and traditional financial markets have different trading calendars and observation times.
- The S&P 500 price index does not include dividend reinvestment.
- Treasury yields are interest-rate levels, not investment returns.
- Google Trends measures relative search interest, not absolute search volume or direct investor sentiment.
- Historical relationships may change over time.
- Historical financial performance does not guarantee future results.
- No forecasting performance or trading profitability is claimed.
